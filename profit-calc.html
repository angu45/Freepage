Why you need to track Project Margins

When you take on a flat-fee project, your biggest risk is **Scope Creep**. If a $2,000 project takes 20 hours, you earn $100/hr. If it takes 40 hours because of "extra edits," you've just cut your pay to $50/hr.

Use this tool to factor in External Costs. If you are hiring a voiceover artist or buying stock photos for a client, that money shouldn't be counted as your profit.
